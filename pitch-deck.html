The opportunity

Three wins, one service

  • A premium guest experience. Valet with an SMS ticket, hands-free shopping and fast, measured pickup — the kind of arrival a flagship centre is judged on.
  • More tenant spend. Guests who hand over their keys and their bags stay longer and spend more — dwell time you can see and grow.
  • Zero operational burden. We run it end to end. The centre gets the reporting, the reputation lift and the data — not another thing to manage.
The guest experience

Hands-free, from kerb to car

SMS ticket

The moment we take the keys, the guest gets a live ticket on their phone — a QR and a "request my car" button. No paper, no kerbside wait.

Hands-free shopping

Guests hand us their bags and keep shopping. We collect, photograph and deliver them to the car — logged round by round.

Pickup on an SLA

A request raises an instant alert and enters the pickup queue, measured against a wait-time target. Nobody waits at the kerb.

For the centre

Live operational visibility

Every car, bay, payment and pickup — in real time, on one dashboard. Tap any number to drill into the cars behind it. The duty manager always knows exactly what's happening on the forecourt, and so can you.

  • Cars on site, pickups pending, today's revenue and average stay — at a glance.
  • Full searchable log and per-staff performance.
  • Card takings reconciled automatically against the terminal.
Demand intelligence

Know your demand before it arrives

Every day's volume, set against the weather and public & school holidays. The pattern is clear and usable: quiet weekdays, busy weekends, and the wet-weather and holiday spikes that drive your busiest trading.

  • Staff to demand — roster to the days that need it, not the days that don't.
  • Time tenant promotions — put activations on the days footfall is already high.
  • Prove the footfall story — a monthly report you can hand to tenants and leasing.
Risk, handled

Every car accounted for

  • Photographed at check-in. A condition record for every vehicle before we move it.
  • Damage logged and acknowledged. Any new damage is recorded and the guest signs off — and the system flags anything left unacknowledged.
  • Secured, access-controlled data. Guest details are never exposed publicly.
  • Never stops. A manual key-tag fallback and an offline queue keep the service running through any outage.
