The 7 beats — what each one says

  1. Check-in — log the car; the guest instantly gets an SMS ticket. "The moment we take the keys, the customer gets this on their phone."
  2. The ticket — QR + "request my car". "They shop completely hands-free and call their car from anywhere in the centre."
  3. Hands-free shopping — log a bag pickup. "Shoppers stay longer and spend more — that's revenue for your tenants."
  4. Pickup, on an SLA — request raises an alert → pickup queue. "No waiting at the kerb — measured, on time."
  5. Live visibility — the dashboard; tap any number to drill in. "Every car, bay, payment and pickup — live."
  6. Demand intelligence — volume vs weather & holidays. "Staff to demand, and time your tenant promotions."
  7. Risk, handled — check-in photos, damage logged & acknowledged. "A premium experience, with the risk managed."

Lead with what management cares about

  • Premium experience — SMS QR ticket, hands-free, fast pickup
  • Tenant spend & dwell — bag service keeps shoppers in-centre
  • Visibility — live dashboard + weather/holiday demand reports
  • Risk handled — incident logs + check-in photos, secured data

Quick answers (anticipate these)

  • Liability / damage? Every car is photographed at check-in and any damage is logged; the guest acknowledges it, and the system flags anything unacknowledged.
  • Customer data / privacy? Names and mobiles are held in a secured, access-controlled cloud and never exposed publicly.
  • If the tech fails? Manual key-tag fallback and an offline queue — the service never stops.
  • Cost to the centre? [your commercial model] — minimal burden; the centre gets the reporting and the reputation lift.
  • Payments? Card via terminal, reconciled automatically; SMS tickets cost a few cents each.
  • Are these numbers real? The demo shows sample data (badged on screen) sized to a realistic flagship-centre pattern. Revenue is shown at a stated per-car fee.

The report is live

You don't need to carry a separate report — the demo's Reports → Monthly Calendar exports to PDF/CSV on the spot, so the leave-behind always matches exactly what you just showed. The management deck and this sheet are for the conversation; the report comes straight from the app.

Before you walk in

  • Charge the device. That's it — no venue wifi, hotspot, SMS credit or card reader required.
  • Have the demo link (or the installed app) ready; do one practice run of the guided tour.
  • Optional: open Auto-play on a second screen at reception so it sells while you talk.
