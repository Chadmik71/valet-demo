Valet outage sheet

Internet down? What to do

Chatswood Chase Valet · keep this at the desk with blank sheets and the pre-numbered paper key tags · owner: text or call if anything here doesn't work
Step 1 (1 minute): is it only the Wi-Fi? Turn on a staff phone's mobile hotspot and connect the tablet to it. If the app goes back online, carry on as normal. Nothing else to do.

Up to about 1 hour: keep using the app

  1. Use ONE device only to log cars in and out. Devices can't see each other while offline, so two devices could put two cars in the same bay.
  2. Keep that device charged and switched on. Don't clear its browser or uninstall the app while the red "not synced" banner shows. Those cars are stored only on that device.
  3. The customer's text won't go automatically. On a phone, send it from Messages (it opens filled in). Otherwise tell the customer their ticket number. They can collect by ticket number or plate.
  4. Square still works on its own data. Write down what each customer paid and whether by card or cash.
  5. When the internet is back, the red banner clears by itself. Check it's gone on every device.

Over 1 hour, or the app won't open: use paper

  1. Paper becomes the record. One row per car on the sheet (page 2), filled in at check-in. Add time out and payment at pickup.
  2. Put a pre-numbered paper key tag on the keys. Write that number in the "Key tag no." column and give the customer the matching stub.
  3. Cars already in the app can still be checked out on the offline device while it stays charged.
  4. At pickup, check the paper row matches the customer's tag number and plate before handing over keys.

When the internet is back

  1. App → New Entry → Enter paper cars. Type each row: date, key tag no., rego, brand, name, mobile, bay, time in. If collected, tick Car already collected and add time out, paid by, fee and tip. Press Save & next.
  2. No text is sent and no key tag prints for these cars. They show PAPER in the Full Log.
  3. Type them in within 7 days (sooner is better). Keep the paper sheet with the day's paperwork.
  4. Dashboard → Closing check: the card total should match Square for the day.
Before every shift: a charged, signed-in spare tablet; this sheet plus blank copies; paper key tags; a pen. Each device should have opened the app once with internet since the last update, so it can open offline.
